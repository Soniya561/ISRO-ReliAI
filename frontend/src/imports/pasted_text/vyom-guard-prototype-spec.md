Design and build an **extraordinary, presentation-ready, fully clickable web prototype** for an aerospace reliability platform called:

# **VYOM-Guard**

### AI-Powered Component Burn-In & Screening Intelligence

**Tagline:**
**“Detect. Predict. Protect.”**

The product is an AI-based system for detecting abnormal behaviour and predicting degradation in electronic components during burn-in and screening tests.

This prototype will be presented to **SIH judges**, so it must look like a serious aerospace engineering product rather than a generic admin dashboard.

---

# VISUAL DIRECTION

Create a **premium LIGHT aerospace theme**.

Do NOT use a dark theme.

Use:

* White as the primary background
* Very light blue / ice-blue sections
* Deep navy for important text
* Saffron/orange as a subtle Indian aerospace accent
* Teal/green for healthy states
* Amber for warnings
* Red only for critical/high-risk states

Use sophisticated gradients only where useful.

The interface should feel inspired by:

* Spacecraft control systems
* Aerospace engineering
* Mission operations
* Scientific instrumentation
* Modern AI products

Avoid:

* Generic SaaS dashboard appearance
* Excessive cards everywhere
* Neon colours
* Cyberpunk styling
* Dark space backgrounds
* Overuse of rockets and planets
* Cartoon graphics

Use **subtle aerospace visual language**, not obvious decoration.

# FINAL DESIGN GOAL

The final prototype should make a judge immediately understand:

**WHAT IS BEING TESTED?**
Electronic components.

**WHAT DATA IS COLLECTED?**
Temperature, voltage, current, resistance and other sensor parameters.

**WHAT DOES AI DO?**
Detects anomalies and predicts degradation.

**WHAT IS THE OUTPUT?**
Normal / Warning / High Risk.

**WHY IS IT USEFUL?**
It helps identify potentially weak components earlier during screening.

The final result must feel like a **real aerospace reliability intelligence platform**, with a clean light interface, sophisticated data visualization, strong AI storytelling, and a highly polished SIH-level presentation experience.
